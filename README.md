# For the Lonely — You Are Seen

A quiet, image-led Scripture page for the lonely — for everyone sitting alone tonight wondering if anyone sees them.

Passages from the World English Bible (public domain) are gathered around God's nearness, presence, and love: He sets the lonely in families (Psalm 68:6), He will never leave nor forsake you (Hebrews 13:5), He is near to all who call on Him (Psalm 145:18), you are fully known and loved (Psalm 139), and there is a friend closer than a brother (Proverbs 18:24).

Built with Muse artifacts.
