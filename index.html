⌂ Home

For the lonely:
you are seen.

For tonight, or any night when the room feels too quiet and you wonder if anyone knows you are there.

The ache of loneliness is real. Scripture does not dismiss it. It draws near and says: God sees you. God stays. God is nearer than the silence.

01

God makes a home for the lonely

“God sets the lonely in families. He brings out the prisoners with singing, but the rebellious dwell in a sun-scorched land.”

Psalm 68:6
02

He will not leave you

“Be free from the love of money, content with such things as you have, for he has said, ‘I will in no way leave you, neither will I in any way forsake you.’”

Hebrews 13:5

“Be strong and courageous. Don’t be afraid or scared of them, for Yahweh your God himself is who goes with you. He will not fail you nor forsake you.”

Deuteronomy 31:6
03

Call to him from where you are

“Yahweh is near to all those who call on him, to all who call on him in truth.”

Psalm 145:18
04

Jesus understands loneliness

“He was despised and rejected by men, a man of suffering and acquainted with disease. He was despised as one from whom men hide their face; and we didn’t respect him.”

Isaiah 53:3

“He came to the disciples and found them sleeping, and said to Peter, ‘What, couldn’t you watch with me for one hour?’”

Matthew 26:40
05

You are fully known and loved

“Yahweh, you have searched me, and you know me. You know my sitting down and my rising up. You perceive my thoughts from afar. You search out my path and my lying down, and are acquainted with all my ways.”

Psalm 139:1–3

“Where could I go from your Spirit? Or where could I flee from your presence? If I ascend up into heaven, you are there. If I make my bed in Sheol, behold, you are there! If I take the wings of the dawn, and settle in the uttermost parts of the sea, even there your hand will lead me, and your right hand will hold me.”

Psalm 139:7–10
06

A friend closer than a brother

“A man of many companions may be ruined, but there is a friend who sticks closer than a brother.”

Proverbs 18:24

You are not unseen in this room. The God who knows every path you take is here—and his hand will hold you.

Scripture quotations from the World English Bible, public domain.
